Dress sellers on ChatGPT ads: a click rate 7x the ChatGPT average

TL;DR

  • A US special-occasion dress brand started product-feed ads on ChatGPT on November 12. In the first three days its two brands combined reached a 4.7% click rate, with daily figures between 3.8% and 5.2%.
  • On the same scale, the ChatGPT ads average is about 0.68% and the top quartile about 1% (Similarweb, May 2026). The Google Shopping average is about 0.86%.
  • A high click rate is not profit. At $0.90 to $1.50 per click this is cheap for ChatGPT but roughly twice what Google Shopping and Meta charge for apparel. Three days show nothing about sales; wait at least two weeks before raising budget.

2026-11-16 · Stan Lu · ChatGPT Ads Update Explainer · Own campaign, 2026-11-12 to 11-14; benchmarks retrieved 2026-11-15

Dresses are an ask-before-you-buy category, and product cards in ChatGPT drew a high click rate. A click rate tells you people looked. It does not tell you the spend pays off.

This covers the first three days of one real campaign. Brand names, spend and traffic volume are not disclosed; only the click rate is shown. Every industry figure is sourced.

1. What ran, and the numbers

  • Brand: a US direct-to-consumer store selling prom, bridesmaid, wedding-guest and formal dresses. Most dresses are listed at $110 to $150.
  • Format: product feed. The ad is a product with a photo and price, shown next to chats like "which bridesmaid dress works for a fall wedding?"
  • Setup: United States, pay per click, one campaign per store, landing pages are product pages, links carry UTM tags.

Both brands combined: 4.7%. Brand A about 4.5%, brand B about 4.8%. Lowest day 3.8%, highest 5.2%.

2. Against the benchmarks

  • ChatGPT average: Similarweb's May 2026 data puts ChatGPT ads near 0.68% overall, about 1% for the top quartile and about 5.4% for the best performers. This campaign sits near the top, not at it.
  • Google Shopping: also a photo-and-price product card. OwlClaw puts the all-category average near 0.86%, with 1.2% and up counted as strong; an older apparel benchmark sits at 0.76%. Same format, about five times the average here.
  • Meta: Lebesgue reports a 2.46% median click rate for fashion brands with $100 to $200 orders. This campaign is about twice that, a smaller gap than against Google.
  • Why so high: my reading, two reasons. Dresses need choosing (style, color, size), and people already ask ChatGPT "which one works?", so a product card answers the question in place. And few retailers advertise on ChatGPT yet; Graphite's report says retail and ecommerce advertisers have been "largely absent", so there are few competing cards.

3. Cost per click is low only inside ChatGPT

The two brands paid about $0.90 and $1.50 per click.

  • Low for ChatGPT: published ecommerce campaigns mostly report $1.70 to $4.40 per click.
  • Higher elsewhere: Google Shopping apparel runs roughly $0.45 to $0.70 per click; Meta fashion about $0.40 to $0.80, and about $0.50 for $100 to $200 orders.

So this is not "ChatGPT is cheaper than Google". The accurate version: about twice the price per click, several times the click rate.

4. What we cannot say yet: profit

Three days show clicks, not sales. Work out the bar first:

Break-even conversion rate = cost per click × target ROAS ÷ order value

At a $130 order:

Cost per clickBreak-evenFor 2x ROAS
Brand B~$0.90~0.7%~1.4%
Brand A~$1.50~1.2%~2.3%

For reference, Lebesgue reports a 1.33% median conversion rate from Meta ads for fashion brands with $100 to $200 orders. Brand B's break-even sits below that and has room; brand A's sits right at it, and 2x return needs 2.3%, so it depends on the landing pages.

Two things can move this:

  • Bridesmaid orders often include several dresses, which raises order value and lowers the bar.
  • In one published Brazil campaign, conversion rate fell 88% by week four, and same-advertiser comparisons show ChatGPT converting below Google. Early numbers do not extrapolate.

5. How dress sellers should run it now

  1. Use product-feed ads, not text ads. Dresses sell on the photo.
  2. One campaign per brand. The two brands' cost per click differed by 60%; mixed together you cannot tell which one deserves more budget.
  3. Set up UTM tags before launch. Source, campaign and ad group, or in two weeks you will have clicks and no sales to read.
  4. Wait two full weeks before raising budget. November is prom off-season; the peak runs January to May.

FAQ

What is the average click rate for ChatGPT ads?

About 0.68% per Similarweb's May 2026 data, about 1% for the top quartile and about 5.4% for the best performers. Search Engine Journal reports that OpenAI's Help Center says there are no cross-advertiser benchmarks yet.

What click rate can dress product-feed ads get on ChatGPT?

In this US campaign, the first three days reached 4.7% across two brands, 3.8% to 5.2% per day. It is one campaign's early result, not a category average.

Are ChatGPT ad clicks cheaper than Google?

Not for apparel. This campaign paid about $0.90 to $1.50 per click; Google Shopping apparel runs about $0.45 to $0.70 and Meta fashion about $0.40 to $0.80. It is only cheaper than other published ChatGPT ecommerce campaigns.

How do I tell whether ChatGPT ads are profitable?

Compute break-even conversion rate = cost per click × target ROAS ÷ order value, then compare it with real orders tracked through UTM tags. Give it at least two weeks.
